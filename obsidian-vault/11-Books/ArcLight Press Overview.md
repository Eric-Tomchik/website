# ArcLight Press Overview

> Eric Tomchik's independent publishing imprint.

---

## About
ArcLight Press is Eric Tomchik's self-publishing imprint. All books are independently written, edited, and published — no traditional publisher involved.

## Publishing Cadence
- 2–4 titles per year (from FAQ)

## Available Formats
| Format | Fulfillment | Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Hardback | Physical | Shipped to address |
| Paperback | Physical | Shipped to address |
| Digital (PDF/ePub) | Instant | Download link via email (72hr, 5 downloads) |

## Payment Methods
- Stripe (credit/debit cards)
- PayPal
- Select titles available on Amazon

## Digital Download Protection
- Time-limited tokens (72 hours)
- Usage-limited (5 downloads per purchase)
- Files watermarked with purchaser info
- Licensed for personal, non-commercial use only

## Companion Resources
- Free companion pages at `/resources`
- Updated directories, pricing tables, tool comparisons
- Keep book content accurate after publication

## Bulk Orders
- Available for 10+ copies
- Contact for custom quote via `/contact`
