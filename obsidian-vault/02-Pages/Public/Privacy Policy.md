## Purpose
Legal privacy policy explaining data collection, usage, and third-party services.

## Sections

### 1. Information We Collect
- **Contact information** — name, email, message via contact form
- **Purchase information** — name, email, shipping address, payment details (Stripe/PayPal handle card data)
- **Account information** — email and password for client portal

### 2. How We Use Information
- Process and fulfill book orders
- Provide client portal and project management access
- Respond to contact form submissions
- Send transactional emails (confirmations, download links)
- Improve website and services

### 3. Third-Party Services
| Service | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Stripe & PayPal | Secure payment processing |
| Convex | Data storage and real-time features |
| Google Analytics | Anonymous website usage analytics |
| Resend | Transactional email delivery |
| Cloudflare | Website hosting and performance |

### 4. Cookies
- Essential cookies for admin/portal sessions
- Google Analytics cookies for anonymous usage data
- Users can disable cookies in browser settings

### 5. Data Security
- Standard security measures to protect personal information

### 6. Contact
- Questions about privacy → contact form or info@erictomchik.com
